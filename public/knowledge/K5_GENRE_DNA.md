# K5 — Genre DNA Library

*Consolidated from P.I.M.P. canonical v1.5 §14, Section 20 templates, the anti-trope genre tables, and the release-pipeline visual rules — 2026-07. Per-genre profiles are new consolidation of fragments scattered across those sources.*

**WHEN TO USE THIS FILE:** establishing a song's genre behavior; designing a fusion (who owns which layer); checking a genre's default template, trope strictness, or visual identity; the user names a genre not yet specced in the conversation.

Genre DNA defines **behavioral traits, not just sound**: rhythm style, instrument palette, harmonic tendencies, vocal character, structural expectations. In fusion, the **Primary Spine** owns structure + rhythm; the **Secondary Color** owns texture, harmony color, or vocal inflection — never both structure and rhythm.

**Profile fields:** Rhythm · Instruments · Harmony · Vocal · Default template (K1) · Trope tier (K2 §7) · Visuals (K4) · Fusion notes.

## Pop (streaming-era)
**Rhythm:** tight, quantized, groove-forward; four-on-the-floor or trap-lite hybrid. **Instruments:** synth beds, processed guitars, programmed drums, sub bass; sonic density in the chorus. **Harmony:** diatonic loops, 4-chord cycles, bright pre-chorus lifts. **Vocal:** polished, forward, stacked harmonies in hooks; conversational verse register. **Template:** Modern Pop (Streaming). **Trope tier:** STRICT — highest cliché risk; Tier 1 in a chorus auto-rewrites. **Visuals:** high-gloss, saturated palette, face-forward composition. **Fusion:** near-universal color layer; as spine it demands the early hook regardless of partner.

## Indie / Alternative
**Rhythm:** looser, human, allowed to drift; syncopation over grid. **Instruments:** character over polish — jangly or textural guitars, analog synths, room-sound drums. **Harmony:** modal color, borrowed chords, unresolved cadences welcome. **Vocal:** idiosyncratic timbre prized over technique; intimacy or detachment as a stance. **Template:** Indie/Alternative (looser forms). **Trope tier:** STRICT — no allowances; flag anything corporate/generic; bias to subversion. **Visuals:** film grain, muted or off-kilter palettes, negative space, anti-glamour. **Fusion:** best as spine with an unexpected color; as color it lends texture and "wrongness."

## Metal / Hard Rock
**Rhythm:** riff-locked; aggressive kick patterns, half-time breakdowns as a weapon. **Instruments:** high-gain guitars as identity modules, double-kick or heavy backbeat drums, low-tuned bass welded to the kick. **Harmony:** minor/modal, power-chord architecture, chromatic tension; minimal harmonic movement is legitimate. **Vocal:** clean/harsh spectrum must be declared (sung, growled, screamed, chanted); dominance or catharsis stance. **Template:** Metal/Hard Rock (riff-module logic — never pop-with-distortion). **Trope tier:** LENIENT — darkness/shadows/blood density allowed; still block Tier 0; flag pop pleas and weak filler. **Visuals:** fire, ash, rust, cracked stone; violent contrast, rim light; heroic scale. **Fusion:** spine that keeps riffs and rhythm; takes orchestral, synthwave, or folk color well.

## Hip-Hop / Trap
**Rhythm:** THE identity layer — bar-structured flow, hi-hat subdivisions, 808 patterns; cadence behavior (behind = swagger, ahead = urgency) is a writing decision. **Instruments:** 808 bass, sparse melodic loops, space as an instrument. **Harmony:** minimal, loop-based; mood from timbre more than progression. **Vocal:** flow identity first; ad-libs as punctuation; sung hooks mark chorus behavior. **Template:** Hip-Hop/Trap (verse-hook, hook-first legal). **Trope tier:** STANDARD — braggadocio, hustle/grind/streets, literal "ice" allowed; flag vague abstraction and generic love-clichés; specificity and literalness are the currency. **Visuals:** location-real or luxury-symbolic; hard contrast, street or studio light. **Fusion:** as spine it owns rhythm AND vocal cadence; as color it lends drum language to pop or R&B.

## Country / Heartland Rock
**Rhythm:** steady 4/4, backbeat honesty, train or shuffle feels. **Instruments:** acoustic/Telecaster guitars, pedal steel, fiddle, real drums; barn-wood warmth. **Harmony:** I-IV-V with relative-minor turns; Nashville clarity. **Vocal:** plainspoken storyteller, regional grain, title-thesis choruses. **Template:** Heartland/Country Rock (story-forward). **Trope tier:** STANDARD — truck/whiskey/rain/mama/small town allowed and logged; flag abstract urban imagery; risk is bro-country regression. **Visuals:** grain, sun fade, golden hour, story-first human warmth. **Fusion:** spine for storytelling hybrids (country-rock, country-trap needs care: country keeps structure + narrative voice).

## R&B / Soul
**Rhythm:** pocket-deep groove, swung sixteenths, space between hits. **Instruments:** electric piano, clean guitar chanks, round bass, tight kit or programmed neo-soul drums. **Harmony:** extended chords (7ths/9ths/13ths), gospel movement, chromatic passing. **Vocal:** the lead instrument — runs, ad-libs, call-and-response, breath detail; vamp outros as release. **Template:** R&B/Soul (vocal space + vamp). **Trope tier:** STANDARD. **Visuals:** warm amber/midnight tones, skin-tone-flattering light, intimate framing. **Fusion:** color layer that gifts harmony and vocal behavior; as spine it demands groove authority.

## EDM / Dance
**Rhythm:** four-on-the-floor or genre-specific grid (house/techno/DnB); the energy contour IS the composition. **Instruments:** synth risers, sidechained pads, sub drops, FX sweeps. **Harmony:** loop-based, tension via filter and arrangement rather than progression. **Vocal:** topline hooks, chopped vocal textures; often sample-like. **Template:** EDM/Dance (build-drop cycles; drop = chorus). **Trope tier:** STANDARD (lyrics are sparse — police the topline hard). **Visuals:** neon bloom, luminous atmosphere, symmetrical scale. **Fusion:** spine that owns rhythm and the build-drop architecture; any melodic world can be its color.

## Cinematic / Trailer (Hybrid Orchestral)
**Rhythm:** hit-based; percussion as impact architecture (taikos, hybrid slams), not groove. **Instruments:** orchestral sections + sound design (braams, risers, sub-booms) + optional rock edge. **Harmony:** modal grandeur, ostinato builds, suspension over resolution until the climax. **Vocal:** optional — choirs, wordless motifs, or a single processed voice. **Template:** Cinematic Trailer (three-act; state the destination early). **Trope tier:** LENIENT — atmospheric motifs allowed; leitmotif repetition is the point. **Visuals:** widescreen scale, atmospheric depth, single monumental subject. **Fusion:** color layer for any genre needing scale (Cinematic Mode); as spine, partners supply texture only.

## Singer-Songwriter / Folk
**Rhythm:** rubato-tolerant, fingerpicked or brushed pulse; the lyric sets the meter's feel. **Instruments:** one anchor instrument (acoustic guitar/piano) + sparse color; room realism. **Harmony:** open-voiced folk shapes, capo colors, gentle suspensions. **Vocal:** close-mic intimacy, breath detail, imperfection as authenticity. **Template:** Singer-Songwriter/Acoustic Ballad. **Trope tier:** STRICT-leaning-STANDARD — sparse arrangements expose lazy lines; concrete detail is the genre's engine. **Visuals:** natural light, handmade texture, solitary human scale. **Fusion:** spine for Ballad Mode; takes ambient or chamber color.

## Dark Americana / Gothic Country
**Rhythm:** slow stomp, funeral waltz, chain-gang pulse. **Instruments:** baritone guitar, banjo/dobro played dark, upright bass, boom-chick percussion, drones. **Harmony:** minor keys, drones, hymn bones under murder-ballad melody. **Vocal:** low, weathered, testimonial; spoken-sung tolerated. **Template:** Heartland base darkened — or Ballad with a stomp; cinematic hooks welcome. **Trope tier:** STANDARD — country allowances apply, plus biblical/gallows imagery allowed when concrete; flag when it drifts to costume. **Visuals:** dust, old wood, fog, lantern dusk, wide lonely frames. **Fusion:** spine for gothic storytelling; color layer that darkens country or rock instantly.

## Synthwave / Retrowave
**Rhythm:** motorik 4/4 or gated 80s backbeat, arpeggiator pulse as the engine. **Instruments:** analog polysynths, FM bells, gated reverb drums, chorused bass. **Harmony:** minor-key nostalgia, suspended pads, filter movement as harmonic breath. **Vocal:** optional; reverb-washed, restrained, or vocoded. **Template:** Modern Pop or EDM chassis with extended instrumental sections. **Trope tier:** STANDARD — but "neon" imagery is this genre's enregistered convention: allowed and logged, still capped by K2 stacking rules. **Visuals:** neon bloom, glass reflection, horizon symmetry, digital haze. **Fusion:** color layer par excellence (metal-synthwave, pop-retrowave); as spine it owns tempo and arp grid.

## Gospel
**Rhythm:** clap-backbeat, swung triplet feels, tempo lifts as testimony builds. **Instruments:** Hammond organ, piano, choir, live kit, tambourine. **Harmony:** rich extended movement, walk-ups, modulation lifts as emotional architecture. **Vocal:** lead + choir call-and-response, ad-lib freedom, vamp endings mandatory equipment. **Template:** R&B/Soul chassis with vamp emphasis; Ritual→Ascension arc native. **Trope tier:** STANDARD — faith vocabulary (grace, glory, water, mountain) is enregistered and allowed when the song earns it concretely; the Borrowed Rule (K2 ADD-1) applies with full force to holiness language. **Visuals:** light rays, soft cloth, air particles, uplifted vertical framing. **Fusion:** color layer that sanctifies anything (gospel-metal, gospel-trap); as spine it owns the vamp and the vocal architecture.

## Fusion quick-reference

| If the pairing is… | Spine owns | Color owns |
|---|---|---|
| Rhythm-genre + melody-genre (trap × R&B) | Drums, bar structure | Harmony, vocal runs |
| Heavy + atmospheric (metal × synthwave) | Riffs, structure, drums | Synth texture, lead colors |
| Story + scale (country × cinematic) | Narrative voice, verse/chorus | Strings, risers, final-chorus scale |
| Sacred + secular (gospel × anything) | Host genre's structure | Choir, vamp, harmonic lifts |

Rule of thumb: if you can't say in one sentence which layer each genre controls, the fusion is decorative — redesign it.
